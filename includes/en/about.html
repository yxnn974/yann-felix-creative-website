About me

Telecommunications student looking for a work-study placement (alternance) for my final year of the BUT, ideally in an engineering design office.

I am in the 3rd year of a BUT in Networks & Telecommunications (a 3-year university bachelor of technology) at the IUT de La Réunion, in the Operator Networks and Multimedia pathway. I am learning what makes the internet, mobile phone networks, optical fibre and radio links work. My goal: to join an engineering design office as a design engineer or a technician specialised in radio and microwave links (radio links that connect two distant points), while staying close to the field.

What I can bring: precision and rigour. During my internship at Texelens, I helped install and maintain optical fibre networks to the home (FTTH): connecting cables, splicing fibres, measuring signal quality and tracing the origin of faults. I also carried out a radio reception project: I set up a radio receiver and decoded the signals sent by aircraft in order to follow air traffic in real time. These projects are detailed on the Projects page.

Besides this, I have a solid grounding in computer networks, system administration (Linux) and cybersecurity (training from ANSSI, the French national cybersecurity agency). Independent and calm when the unexpected happens, I can work alone as well as in a team.
